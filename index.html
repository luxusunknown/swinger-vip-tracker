Range of possible outcomes

A simplified hypothetical, not a replay of real trades: it applies each call's real % return to a risk amount sized YOUR way (not the analyst's own dollar figures — we don't know their real position size, only their % move). "Blend" pools every selected analyst's calls into one shared account in the order they actually happened; "Compare" gives each analyst their own independent account from the same starting capital. "Skip calls I couldn't afford" is the closest this gets to real-world whole-contract limits — off by default, it assumes fractional sizing, which is optimistic. Slippage/fees haircut every modeled return to account for real execution. "Split evenly" is a fixed baseline: your starting capital divided equally across every tracked analyst, each run independently and added back together. "Range of possible outcomes" reruns these exact same trades in hundreds of random orders — the ending number is often identical for %-based sizing (multiplying the same returns in a different order gives the same product), but the bumpiness of the ride and the odds of an early unlucky stretch busting the account are very real and very order-dependent. Use this to compare scenarios, not as a literal "you would have made $X" prediction. Historical simulation only — not financial advice.
